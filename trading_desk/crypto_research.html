SEPARATE ML RESEARCH / ENTRY PATTERNS

Winning vs losing entries · ML quality filter

No live promotion or orders

Reading saved entry-quality research…

Negative result; keep disabled. The fixed 0.50 classifier rejects 16 of 17 original BTC/SOL test winners while avoiding 10 of 12 losses. Test AUC is about 0.412 and prediction error is worse than the train-only asset-frequency baseline. Full sequential test P/L also deteriorates. This filter learns entry features; it does not retune ATR, coin weights, stops or exits.

A regularized logistic model compares completed candle shape, recent trend, recovery from D, D age, chase, pullback depth, entry type and asset. No future candle, exit, realized P/L, volume finalized after entry or target is a model input. Scores are research outputs, not calibrated win odds. A binary win label ignores payoff magnitude; improved win-rate classification alone would not establish better returns.

RESEARCH ONLY / BTC BASELINE + SOL WEIGHT

Adaptive crypto shocks & chronological calibration

Frozen 0.2 and live workers unchanged

Reading the saved adaptive comparison…

Mixed results; no promotion. A shallower BTC threshold improves on the strict 3-ATR draft in this sample. SOL's extra 1.5 weight reduces full-month return but improves the last nine days. Calibration selected weight 1.0 before the test, and did not outperform ATR-only scaling. These histories were studied previously: this is a retrospective holdout, not unseen validation. The adaptive drafts do not beat the original frozen full-month totals.

Use ATR% to compare different price levels. BTC weight is fixed at 1.0; the SOL weight is an extra strictness multiplier, not statistical beta. Threshold = BTC base × BTC ATR% × max(1, asset ATR% / BTC ATR%) × coin weight. This raises SOL's required percentage drop when SOL is more volatile. A non-shock must still pass A/P1 structure; otherwise wait.
