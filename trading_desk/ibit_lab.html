IBIT trades on an exchange schedule

Only exchange-calendar regular minutes are included: normally 9:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. New York time, Monday–Friday, with holidays, early closes and daylight saving handled by actual session times. Premarket and after-hours bars are excluded.

Positions can carry overnight. There is no forced daily sale. Stops and other exits can act only on the next actual regular-session price; a gap through a stop uses the observed opening reference. No overnight stop protection or closing-auction fill is assumed.

The exact BTC rules cannot form their continuous multi-hour resistance structure on this regular-session tape, producing zero trades. The separate adapted arm forms one trading-hour block from four actual complete 15-minute bars, and a four-trading-hour block from sixteen. These derived blocks can span overnight closures: they are trading-time blocks, not ordinary wall-clock 1h/4h chart candles. Native minute timestamps are retained; no overnight prices or bars are invented.

Peak confirmation, resistance prominence/zones, target buffer, entry and exit thresholds, parent-D protection and 25%/75% sales retain their original settings. Closed intervals do not count as missing observations in the adapted arm. Original 15-minute entry structures still reset at overnight wall-clock discontinuities; a stale prior-day bar cannot trigger a 9:30 opening buy.

Each illustrative account starts with $10,000 and reinvests available cash after a position exits. Fixed-$10,000-entry P/L is separate. Costs retain the 2bps/side ETF spread/slippage proxy; actual spreads, market impact and fills are unverified. Drawdown uses observed minute-close liquidation marks and can miss worse intraminute or off-hours declines. This is a previously studied, retrospective period with few trades.
