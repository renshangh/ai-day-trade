Pattern decisions

A valid B1 crossing must pass the inherited structure/risk checks, complete context and freshness, be within the reference-library radius, then be strictly closer to a winning reference than a losing lookalike. The radius is the largest nearest-neighbor distance when each winning example is held out in turn. A skipped signal is never a fill. The paper book allows one position per symbol.
