Pattern matches and skipped signals
The unchanged model requires 14 consecutive full 15-minute context bars. Overnight stock closures break that continuity, so morning entries can be rejected even with complete regular-session data. Ordinary closures are excluded from required stock-minute coverage; the inherited engine can still flag them as gaps. Actual missing-session-minute counts are separate. An in-window split uses vendor split-adjusted minutes; a missing adjusted series stays unavailable. The unchanged $0.01 trigger increment applies on adjusted prices, without claiming raw historical tick/fill parity.
