# AI Trading Desk v3
Layered market and research desk built over the existing sector board.
Last Updated: 2026-09-27
Status: Development
Audience: Trading Desk users and developers

## Overview

Run `python3 trading_desk/server.py` from the dev checkout and open `http://127.0.0.1:8799/`. The existing sector board remains at `/board`, with its existing API-backed views and charts. The v3 page combines the CIO Brief, scorecard, AI Economy model, AI Coins & Agent Economy, ten report sections, Research Lab, Thesis Tracker, Opportunity Pipeline, and Decision Log.

A dated report snapshot drives the CIO Brief. The AI Economy Score is an equal-weight average of six analyst-entered 0–100 component assessments and appears only when every component has a source URL. The other scores are manual assessments that require a source or method note. Top 5 Changes are sourced developments entered for a report after a baseline report. No current market values are seeded or inferred.

The Altcoin Health Monitor combines eight sourced analyst assessments using the displayed weights. It has no score until all eight are present. The AI Coin Dashboard loads real daily bars from the existing `/api/crypto` route one asset at a time and labels each feed and last bar date. It calculates 1-bar and 7-bar close changes only from returned bars. Missing data stays unavailable. TVL, ETF flows, relative strength, news, and institutional activity require separate sourced observations; they are not inferred from price.

Thesis reviews record dated confidence, trend, supporting evidence, contradicting evidence, and a source link. Opportunity stages and decision outcomes can be updated. The Agent Economy, AI × Blockchain, RWA, Early Signals, and Research Lab panels accept dated sourced observations. Reports, reviews, opportunities, decisions, and observations are saved by the local server to ignored, private `trading_desk/v3_state.json`; a browser recovery copy and JSON export are also available. Writes use a revision check so an older tab cannot silently overwrite newer work. The old preview browser key is read for migration into v3 when the server has no data. Watchlists and research themes are structural examples, not current recommendations.
