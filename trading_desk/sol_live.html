SOL 0.1 entry rules

Measure a complete bearish new-low shock by its net close decline ≥ 2 × SOL ATR14, using fifteen consecutive complete prior 15-minute candles. No other asset's price, ATR or weight is used.

B1 needs a separate completed 15-minute candle after D confirmation to hold D, followed by a break above the previous complete candle's high. A and B2 keep the original structural definitions. The larger-D stop, targets, E1 exits, first 25% sale and remaining 75% exits retain their original settings. A target below 2R remains advisory.

The four comparisons were declared before replay. The combined candidate stays model 0.1 regardless of which historical result is largest. No parameter search or automatic promotion.
