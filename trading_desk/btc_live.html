The worker requests completed 1-minute bars every 15 seconds. All timestamps below use New York time with an explicit zone. A signal's historical minute is different from the time it became observable to this service.

Operating mode and recovery

The service starts collecting forward signals from the next full minute after activation, after seven days of actual warmup observations. Restart replays its saved observations and deduplicates events. Warmup does not open positions. Missed observations stay absent; delayed catch-up events retain their actual detection time and are not retroactive fills.

Pause stops observation processing. Model holdings are simulated; no broker protection or order is running. Resume reconstructs the same session and labels delayed observations. The worker runs separately from this browser while the host is awake and the external disk is available.

Current BTC quotes are references only. The frozen 2bps-per-side cost proxy does not include actual BTC trading fees and must not be interpreted as an executable crypto return.
